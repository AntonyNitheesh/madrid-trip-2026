TOLEDO • 04 OCT

Toledo Old Town

From Madrid: High-speed train from Atocha takes about 30 minutes.

Suggested route: Cathedral → Jewish Quarter → San Juan de los Reyes → Santa María la Blanca → Santo Tomé → Alcázar → Mirador del Valle.

Free: Old-town streets, viewpoints and exterior sightseeing. Individual monuments are ticketed.

SEGOVIA • 05 OCT

Aqueduct • Cathedral • Alcázar

From Madrid: High-speed train from Chamartín to Segovia-Guiomar; then local bus/taxi into the centre.

Free: Aqueduct exterior, historic streets and many viewpoints. Cathedral/Alcázar interiors are ticketed.

ABU DHABI • 07 OCT

Sheikh Zayed Grand Mosque

Best use of your layover: Make this your main Abu Dhabi landmark.

From Zayed International Airport: Taxi/Careem is the most time-efficient for a long layover. Abu Dhabi's official tourism service also lists a free Experience Abu Dhabi shuttle on some routes; check the current airport service/schedule before relying on it.

Entry: FREE. Free guided tours are also available. Advance visit booking is recommended.

Allow: about 1.5–2 hours including entry/security.

Important: Dress modestly; shoulders and knees must be covered.

ABU DHABI • FREE

Wahat Al Karama

Why visit: UAE national memorial opposite the Grand Mosque, making it a natural second stop.

Travel: 🚕 Short taxi ride from the Grand Mosque, or use the pedestrian connection/shuttle options when operating.

Entry: FREE.

Hours: Visitor centre 09:00–19:00; memorial plaza is open 24 hours according to the official tourism listing.

ABU DHABI • FREE

Heritage Village

Location: Corniche Breakwater near Marina Mall.

Why visit: Traditional souk, mosque, desert camp and demonstrations of Emirati crafts such as pottery, glassblowing and weaving.

Travel: 🚕 Taxi/Careem from central Abu Dhabi; combine it with Corniche and Founder’s Memorial.

Entry: FREE as a heritage visit; check the day's operating hours/events before going.

Recommended: 45–90 minutes.

ABU DHABI • FREE

The Founder's Memorial

Why visit: Beautiful cultural memorial dedicated to Sheikh Zayed, with The Constellation artwork.

Travel: 🚕 Taxi/Careem; combine with Heritage Village and Corniche.

Entry: FREE for all.

Hours: 09:00–22:00 daily. Complimentary 30-minute cultural tours are available; individual visitors generally do not need advance booking.

ABU DHABI • FREE

Abu Dhabi Corniche + Public Beach

Why visit: 8 km waterfront promenade, skyline views, gardens and sunset.

Travel: 🚕 Taxi/Careem; easy to combine with Heritage Village and Founder’s Memorial.

Entry: FREE for the public beach and waterfront. The Al Sahil section (Gate 4) is listed as free entry; other beach sections may have a fee.

Best time: Late afternoon/sunset or evening.

ABU DHABI • FREE

Qasr Al Hosn + House of Artisans + Cultural Foundation

Why visit: Best compact heritage stop in central Abu Dhabi. See the House of Artisans, Cultural Foundation and outdoor Al Hosn spaces.

Travel: 🚕 Taxi/Careem from Corniche, or local bus.

Free: House of Artisans, Cultural Foundation and outdoor spaces are complimentary. Qasr Al Hosn fort interior: paid, currently starting at AED 30.

Best for: Culture without spending on a museum ticket.

ABU DHABI • OPTIONAL PAID

Louvre Abu Dhabi

Why visit: Excellent if your layover remains comfortable after immigration, transport and the free cultural stops.

Travel: 🚕 Taxi/Careem to Saadiyat Island.

Entry: Paid; verify the current AED ticket price and opening hours before travel.

Priority: Secondary — do not let it make you rush the airport return.

ABU DHABI • FREE

Free Abu Dhabi Shuttle / Budget Option

Experience Abu Dhabi promotes free shuttle-bus options connecting selected tourist areas. Routes and departure times can change, so check the official schedule shortly before your 07 Oct layover.

Best strategy: Use taxi/Careem for the time-critical airport-to-city and city-to-airport legs; use a free shuttle only when its timetable fits your layover.
