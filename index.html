👔 Clothing strategy & daily combinations

A practical rotation using the same three formal trousers, two jeans and one casual trouser. You do not need a different pair of trousers every day.

BUSINESS DAY 1 • 28 SEP

White shirt + Navy trousers + formal shoes

Clean first-day combination. Use the black belt.

BUSINESS DAY 2 • 29 SEP

Light Blue shirt + Charcoal trousers + formal shoes

Repeat the shoes and belt; change the shirt and trousers.

BUSINESS DAY 3 • 30 SEP

White shirt + Black trousers + formal shoes

Simple formal combination for the middle of the conference week.

BUSINESS DAY 4 • 01 OCT

Light Blue shirt + Navy trousers + formal shoes

Reuses the navy trousers with a different shirt.

BUSINESS DAY 5 • 02 OCT

Light Grey shirt + Charcoal trousers + formal shoes

Finish the business week with a lighter combination.

MADRID EVENING 1 • 28 SEP

Black T-shirt + Light Blue Denim + casual/walking shoes

Gran Vía / Plaza de España.

MADRID EVENING 2 • 29 SEP

Olive T-shirt + White trousers + walking shoes

Prado + Cibeles.

MADRID EVENING 3 • 30 SEP

Navy T-shirt + Black / Dark Denim + walking shoes

Reina Sofía + Atocha / Lavapiés.

MADRID EVENING 4 • 01 OCT

Grey T-shirt + Light Blue Denim + casual shoes

Royal Palace area + old Madrid.

MADRID EVENING 5 • 02 OCT

White T-shirt + White trousers + walking shoes

Retiro + Salamanca.

03 OCT • FULL MADRID

White or Navy T-shirt + Dark Blue jeans + walking shoes

Comfort is the priority for a full sightseeing day.

04 OCT • TOLEDO

Olive T-shirt + White trousers + walking shoes + light jacket

Comfortable for walking on Toledo's streets and viewpoints.

05 OCT • SEGOVIA

Grey T-shirt + Light Blue Denim + walking shoes + light jacket

Good for a full day around the Aqueduct, Old Town and Alcázar.

06 OCT • FINAL MADRID

Black T-shirt + Black / Dark Denim + casual shoes

Easy final-day outfit before the evening flight.

07 OCT • ABU DHABI

White/Light Grey T-shirt + lightweight trousers or shorts + comfortable shoes

Choose the lightest option for the warmer Abu Dhabi weather; keep the outfit accessible during the layover.

Packing principle: The formal trousers and shoes are reused during the business week; the same Light Blue Denim, Black/Dark Denim and White trousers are reused for evenings and sightseeing. This keeps the suitcase lighter without sacrificing variety.
⚠️ Important reminders
UAE: confirm your visa/entry permission before leaving Abu Dhabi airport.

Recheck museum free-entry periods, attraction hours and train schedules shortly before the trip.
