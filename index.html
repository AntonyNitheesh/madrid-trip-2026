TOLEDO • 04 OCT

Toledo Old Town

From Madrid: High-speed train from Atocha takes about 30 minutes.

Suggested route: Cathedral → Jewish Quarter → San Juan de los Reyes → Santa María la Blanca → Santo Tomé → Alcázar → Mirador del Valle.

Free: Old-town streets, viewpoints and exterior sightseeing. Individual monuments are ticketed.

SEGOVIA • 05 OCT

Aqueduct • Cathedral • Alcázar

From Madrid: High-speed train from Chamartín to Segovia-Guiomar; then local bus/taxi into the centre.

Free: Aqueduct exterior, historic streets and many viewpoints. Cathedral/Alcázar interiors are ticketed.

ABU DHABI • 07 OCT • LAYOVER PLAN

Morning → Airport Return Schedule

Arrival: 06:00–08:00 — Clear immigration, collect/leave luggage as arranged with the complimentary Etihad hotel, and travel light.

08:30–10:30 — Sheikh Zayed Grand Mosque: Main landmark of the layover. Entry is FREE; advance visit booking is recommended. Dress modestly and allow time for security.

10:30–11:30 — Wahat Al Karama: Visit the memorial and reflective-pool views. FREE.

12:00–13:00 — Qasr Al Hosn area: Visit the FREE House of Artisans, Cultural Foundation galleries and outdoor Al Hosn spaces. The Qasr Al Hosn fort interior is paid if you choose to enter.

13:00–13:45 — Budget lunch: Shawarma, falafel or a supermarket meal around the downtown/Qasr Al Hosn area.

14:00–15:00 — Founder's Memorial + Corniche/Public Beach: Visit the free memorial and continue to the waterfront. Public areas are FREE.

15:30–16:45 — Shopping: Choose one shopping stop — Madinat Zayed Shopping Centre or Marina Mall. Keep this short to protect your airport buffer.

16:45 — Collect luggage: Return to the complimentary Etihad hotel/luggage-storage point and collect all bags before leaving for the airport.

17:00–18:15 — Airport transfer: A1 Airport Express Bus if its current service/timing fits; otherwise use a taxi for the more predictable journey. Aim to reach Zayed International Airport by 18:15.

18:15 — Airport: This gives approximately 2.5 hours before the planned 20:45 flight.
